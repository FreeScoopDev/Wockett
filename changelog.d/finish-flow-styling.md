### Changed
- Finishing an outing no longer looks like losing it. Red now means lose,
  not save: the Hold to finish button beside Pause was red outline, red text
  and a red fill while held, and in the confirmation that follows "Finish
  and save" was a red outline next to a red "Discard this walk", so the way
  to keep the outing and the way to throw it away looked alike. The hold
  button now has the quiet secondary look (raised fill, dark text, a thin
  grey outline so it still reads as something to press) and fills green as
  you hold it; it still looks nothing like the filled green Pause. In the
  confirmation, "Finish and save" is the filled main button at the top,
  "Keep walking" (or running, riding…) is the quieter button under it, and
  only Discard is red. The one-second hold, the confirmation itself, the
  haptics and what VoiceOver says and does are unchanged.
- At the largest accessibility text sizes the confirmation opens full height
  instead of at its fixed height, which cut its title short ("Finish
  you…"); the title now wraps, centred.
