### Changed
- The launch splash is the app icon come to life: the icon's forest green with its contour lines, and the icon's W drawing itself — the cream road and its dashed centre line along the line, the two orange waypoints settling onto the road as the drawing reaches them, and the rounded arrowhead last. Joe, 2026-10-08: the splash's W ("a thin dashed line with the dots sitting low like saggy rain pillows") did not match the icon. The W is now traced from the 1024 px icon (road 100 px with a 3 px edge, corner radii 57/48/62, waypoints on the centre line, arrowhead corners measured) into one shared `WockettMarkView`, so the splash and the App Store artwork draw the same mark; the contour lines come from `ContourLinesView`, a fixed procedural terrain traced with marching squares (3 ms in a release build). The launch screen (`LaunchBackground`) is the same forest green, so launch → splash is seamless; the splash fades into Home. The icon's palette is in `DesignSystem.swift` as `brandForest`, `brandRoad`, `brandContour`, `brandDash` and `brandWaypoint`, fixed in light and dark. With Reduce Motion on, the W appears already drawn. Checked from simulator recordings.
