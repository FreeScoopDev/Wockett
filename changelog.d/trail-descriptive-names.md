### Changed
- Trail pack builder 1.3.1 treats names that describe a path rather than name it as unnamed: "abandoned track", "abandoned road", "Logging Road", "WMA Road", "Jeep Trail", "Tank Trail", "Field Road", "Access Road", "Forest Service Road", "Fire Road", "Wildlife Planting", "Shortcut" and "Cut Through". Found building the first South Carolina, Virginia, Tennessee and Georgia packs (2026-10-08): "abandoned track" was 46 rows in Georgia and "Logging Road" 37 across four states, each listed as if it were a trail to choose. As unnamed paths they join the corridors beside them, take a road-derived name where one fits, and fall under "Other paths" when short. Exact matches only, so "Logging Road Trail" keeps its name. North Carolina's live pack has 4 such rows and is not rebuilt for this. 1 new builder test; without the new names it goes red.
