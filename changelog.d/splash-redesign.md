### Changed
- The launch splash moves onto the 2026-09-30 design: the app's own background (light or dark, with the phone), the dashed W in Wockett green with orange waypoints, "Wockett" in the shared heavy rounded type, and the tip of the day in a standard card with a "Tip of the day" label. The old forest-green splash was a hard cut on both sides: iOS first shows a blank launch screen (white or black), then the green splash, then a light or dark Home. The launch screen now has the same colour as the splash (`LaunchBackground`, matching `earthBg` in both modes), so opening Wockett is one surface from the first frame to Home. Joe chose this direction over keeping the green, 2026-10-06.
- The W draws itself more smoothly. It used to run on four separate timers (the line, each orange dot, the arrowhead), and trimming a dashed line revealed it a whole dash at a time, so the front jumped. Now one eased progress value drives everything: a small pen dot travels with the tip of the line, each waypoint settles in as the pen passes its valley (measured on the real path), and the arrowhead lands at the end. With Reduce Motion on, the W appears already drawn and the title and tip fade without moving. Checked from simulator recordings in light and dark, sampled every 0.1 s.
- The daily tips are rewritten. Several named things that no longer exist ("Recommend", "Explore", Free Walk mode, the ↑ ↓ elevation numbers on route cards, water-break reminders in Settings). Every tip that names a screen or control was checked against the app on 2026-10-06, and new ones cover Siri, the Control Center button, auto-resume, trail directions and the widget.
