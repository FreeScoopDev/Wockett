### Fixed
- The Apple Health permission text now says what Wockett reads. It said only "Wockett reads your step count", but the app also reads walking distance, flights climbed, active calories, sleep, resting and walking heart rate, heart rate variability, and walking speed, step length and symmetry (Recovery and Walking health). App Review can reject a permission string that undersells what is accessed; the 1.14 release check flagged it. The write text now names runs as well as walks and rides. Every type listed was checked against the `readTypes` in `StepManager`, `RecoveryService` and `GaitHealthView`; the text makes no privacy claim, since joining a community challenge uploads your step totals.
