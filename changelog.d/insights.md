### Added
- Health → Insights: weekly and monthly trends from the walks, runs and rides recorded in Wockett, the first piece of the Premium bundle Joe approved on 2026-10-08. Free, as decided on 2026-09-15 ("a user's own week is their own data"): the period's distance, compared with the same stretch of last week or month (Wednesday noon against last Wednesday noon, so a week in progress is not always "down"), a day-by-day chart, and activities, active time, estimated steps and active days. The depth that `ProFeature.advancedAnalytics` will gate: the last 8 weeks or 6 months, the walk/run/ride mix, the busiest weekday and usual time, and each pet's distance and walks. Pro gating is still off, so everyone sees all of it until the paywall ships. Weeks follow the person's calendar (Sunday in the US), sessions flagged as possible driving are left out, and the numbers come from session history, so they match Activity History and work without Health access.
