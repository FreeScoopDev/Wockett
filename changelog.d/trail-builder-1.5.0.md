### Changed
- Trail pack builder 1.5.0, the second clean-up round, from the review lists the 1.4.0 builds printed (2026-10-09):
  - One trail, one name. Kentucky listed one trail as "Sheltowee Trace", "Sheltowee Trace Trail", "Sheltowee Trace #100" and "Sheltowee Trace Trail #100". Names that differ only by case, spacing or a trailing "Trail" (when two words remain) are now one trail's name. A trailing trail number stays part of the identity: "Loop #1" and "Loop #2", Florida's Beach Access #1 to #14 and Kentucky's Limestone #109 and its spurs #109A-D stay separate trails, never joined, even through an unnamed-number piece between them.
  - A trail's pieces carry one name: the spelling covering most of its length, counted over the original ways even after they are joined, and written properly ("High Falls Loop", not "HIgh Falls Loop"; "Croatan", not "croatan"; single-spaced; "Raider Camp Trail" over a 437 m "Raider Camp").
  - A connector named for what it reaches ("Beltline Access Line", 63 ways averaging 92 m in Atlanta) is unnamed. Names ending "Access Trail" keep their names: real trails carry them ("Hazel Creek Access Trail", 1.1 km).
  - A critic review caught the first version joining differently numbered trails (Florida's Beach Access #1-#4 relabelled "#0") before any of it was published; fixed, and each case checked in the rebuilt packs.
  - Coverage is unchanged in every state against the published baseline. The bundled North Carolina pack is rebuilt with it (6,354 rows).
  - 14 new builder tests; each rule, removed, turned one red. Two critic runs found the naming problems above in the packs before anything was published.
