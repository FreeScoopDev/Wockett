### Added
- **Climb** on outdoor walks, runs and rides: how far up you went, with how far down beneath it, on the summary and again from Activity History, in feet in the US and metres elsewhere. A hiker's first question after a walk is "how much climbing was that?", and Wockett could not answer it: nothing read the altitude every GPS fix already carries. It is worked out on the phone during the activity from those same fixes, so it works offline and costs no extra battery (no new location request, no higher accuracy, no barometer yet). Fixes with an unknown altitude or one worse than 15 m are ignored, and a change counts only once it passes 3 m, so GPS altitude drifting while you stand still adds nothing; after a gap in fixes the level starts again rather than counting the jump. The figure is final when the activity ends: nothing "corrects" it afterwards against a map, which is what people report doubling their gain in other apps. The tile appears only from 5 m of climb; an activity with no usable altitude shows none, so "no data" never reads as "flat". The climb so far survives the app being closed mid-walk. Measured only in tests and the simulator, which has no real altitude; a walk up a real hill is still owed.
