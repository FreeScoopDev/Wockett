### Fixed
- The privacy manifest now says what Community collects. Shared routes (their full GPS line), achievement posts, challenges and challenge entries (step totals, some read from HealthKit) are written to the CloudKit public database, which the developer can read, and each record carries the generated nickname, CloudKit's creator user record ID, and on challenge entries an app-made device ID. So Precise Location, Health and Fitness are linked to the person, and Other User Content, User ID and Device ID are collected, all for App Functionality. Tracking stays No: nothing goes to advertisers or data brokers. Found by an audit against the App Store label on 2026-10-08 (read from source); the App Store Connect label is changed to match, and its Usage Data and Diagnostics entries removed, since nothing sends either.
