### Added
- **Trail facts** on a trail's detail screen: how hard the path is ("Walking path", "Rough mountain path", "Steep, demanding path", "Alpine route, experienced hikers"), how smooth the surface is, how well it is marked, whether it is mapped as wheelchair accessible, whether it is lit at night, and who maintains it. Every trail pack already carried these from the map data and no screen showed them; more about each trail was Joe's first want, and this costs no pack rebuild, no iCloud change and no download. A fact appears only when the map data states it, so most trails show no card at all (of the named trails in the published state packs, from about one in three in Maine and one in four in North Carolina down to one in thirteen in Florida state at least one): a hidden fact is better than a guessed one, and nothing is inferred from a trail's length. A trail made of several sections shows the cautious value where they differ (the hardest path type, the roughest surface, the faintest marking, as the dog rule shows the strictest), and shows wheelchair access, lighting and maintainer only when every section that states one agrees. A line under the card says the facts are from the map data, not a current report.

### Internal
- `TrailFacts` (`Services/Trails/TrailFacts.swift`) turns a trail's pack rows into the facts, as a plain function with its own tests (`TrailFactsTests`), including real rows read from the bundled North Carolina pack.
