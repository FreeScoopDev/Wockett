### Added
- Trail regions for Vermont (probe: Burlington), New Hampshire (Manchester), Maine (Portland), Massachusetts (Boston), Connecticut (Hartford) and Rhode Island (Providence) in `tools/regions.json`, so `make_region.sh` can build them. All six passed the release gates on 2026-10-14 with builder 1.5.0, built as v1 with `LIVE_VERSION=0` because the CloudKit token had expired and none of them has been published. Coverage (cities and towns with a named trail within 3 miles): Vermont 97%, New Hampshire 95%, Maine 95%, Massachusetts 98%, Connecticut 94%, Rhode Island 97%. Not yet published to Development or Production.
