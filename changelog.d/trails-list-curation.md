### Changed
- The Trails list is named places only. Measured over 1,563 towns in the ten published states (2026-10-08), 50-77% of a town's list was unnamed paths or dirt roads, mostly long ones that #146's half-mile rule kept in view. Every unnamed path now sits under "Other paths" ("3 paths with no name"), whatever its length. Joe agreed 2026-10-08.
- A bike path says "Bike path" on its card instead of "Bikes OK" (Joe: "if it is specifically a bike path, we should list it as such"). Walk mode leaves out paths mapped as closed to walkers; Ride still lists them. This relies on packs built with builder 1.4.0, which stops marking every bike path without a foot tag as closed to walkers; the bundled North Carolina pack in this change is rebuilt with it.
- One trail listed twice nearby is one row. The pack builder keys a trail's pieces within 400 m, so a trail with a longer gap arrived as two rows (Greenville's Swamp Rabbit Trail Green Line, Cary's Chapel Hill Road Path). Rows of one name within 1.5 km in the same list now merge; two parks' trails of one name further apart stay separate.
