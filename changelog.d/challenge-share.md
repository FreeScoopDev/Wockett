### Added
- **Share a challenge.** A challenge's screen has a Share Challenge button
  that sends an invitation through the iOS share sheet (Messages, Mail,
  social apps): the challenge's name, its goal and length in plain words
  ("run 10 km in 7 days", "3 runs at 8:00/km pace or faster in 14 days"),
  the App Store link, and where to find it in the app (Community →
  Challenges). Until now the only way into a challenge was to come across it
  in the list, so a challenge could not be used to invite friends. Distance
  and pace use the sharer's units, and the message says "my" challenge only
  to the person who created it. A link that opens the challenge directly is
  a separate change.
