### Added
- Once you have chosen a starting place in Routes (with location off or
  restricted), Trails lists the trails around that place too, instead of
  saying it has no location. The headings name it ("Trails near Pullen Park",
  "Featured near Pullen Park") and a trail's detail says how far it is "from
  Pullen Park", so a distance never reads as if it were from where you are.
  Directions to a trail, how far away it is and the walk plan still start from
  your own location only, since a route that begins somewhere you aren't can't
  be driven or walked from here; with location off they keep saying so.
