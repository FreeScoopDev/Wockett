### Changed
- Walking part of a long trail. A line trail's Start Walk used to plan the walk to the trail's farther end: on a long greenway, a 10 or 30 mile one-way route that ended far from the car, so people would stop partway and leave a route they never finished. Joe, 2026-10-09: "we can't expect the users to complete the entire trail if they are long… It should be somewhat seamless."
  - A line trail is now walked out and back, ending where it began. The trail screen offers Distance | Time: round trips of 1, 2, 3 or 5 mi (2, 3, 5 or 8 km), or 20, 30, 45 or 60 minutes at the person's own recent pace (the median of their last 20 walks long enough to judge, within sensible bounds; a typical pace until there are some), and "End and back" for the whole trail. A loop offers the full loop and the same round trips. The last choice is remembered, and also used when someone navigates to a trail and starts it on arrival.
  - At the turnaround the walk says so: "Turn around here", with the distance back, as a spoken cue and a notification.
  - "Head back now", during a trail walk that is still heading out, turns the rest of the walk into the trail back to where it started, in the same session, after a confirmation.
  - The turnaround survives a crash-and-restore with the walk; older snapshots restore without one.
  - 11 new tests. Breaking the half-distance turnaround, the turnaround rule, the Head back rule, the "shorter than the whole trail" filter, or the pace bounds turned tests red. Not covered by a test: that the live walk calls the turnaround check as GPS fixes arrive (that needs a simulated walk).
