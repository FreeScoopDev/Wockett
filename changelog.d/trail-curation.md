### Changed
- Trails read more like trails and less like map data (pack builder 1.3.0, and the bundled North Carolina pack rebuilt with it). Joe, 2026-10-08: "I don't want there to be a bunch of trail segments for one trail listed as individual trails." Measured on the NC download pack (59,902 rows before), each rule with its own tests and a deliberate break that turned them red:
  - **Pieces of one trail across short gaps join.** Most pieces of a long trail did not touch — a bridge, boardwalk or road crossing between them is tagged as a different way — so end-to-end merging never saw them (of Neuse River Trail's piece ends, 22 sat within 50 m of the next piece and 2 within 1 m). Two ends of the same name now join when they are within 50 m, face each other, and the gap runs the way the trail is heading; never into a ring. 446 joins statewide; Neuse River Trail 29 → 19 pieces.
  - **A trail carries straight on through a spur.** Where three or more pieces of one name meet, the two that continue nearly straight (under 45°, and at least 15° straighter than any other way there) join; a symmetric Y is a fork and is not guessed. 259 joins.
  - **Every piece of a named trail carries a trail key** (`trails.trail_key`: the region and the trail's smallest source ref), grouping the pieces the app groups (same name within 400 m), so the app can show a whole trail rather than the pieces near you. Additive column; the pack schema stays 1 and older apps read packs by column name.
  - **Generic names count as unnamed**: "Trail", "Service Road", "Connector", "Public Beach Access" and 30 other names that say what a path is, not which. 332 rows; they join the unnamed corridors or take a road's name instead.
  - **Unnamed stubs under 150 m are dropped** (loops kept; after road-derived names, so nothing that earned a name goes): 24,900 rows, most of the "38% of unnamed pieces under 100 m".
  - Result on the NC download pack: 59,902 → 34,422 rows, 19.1 → 12.6 MB; named trails in pieces 16% → 13%; pieces under 100 m 34% → 3%. The bundled named-only pack: 9,328 → 8,407 rows; in Routes → Trails near Raleigh, Little Rock Trail 8 → 4 sections, Rocky Branch Trail 10 → 6. Remaining splits are mostly mountain-bike networks (Sherman Branch) and gaps over 50 m, which the trail key ties together.
- Tests look real trails up by source ref (`BundledTrailSource.trail(sourceRef:)`), not by row id, which every rebuild renumbers; the app itself never stored row ids (saved and crash-restored trail walks carry the trail's geometry).
