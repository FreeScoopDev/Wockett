### Changed
- The privacy policy now describes how much 1.16 actually trims from a
  shared route. Its first wording said the first and last 200 m measured
  along the route. The app leaves off at least that much: every point within
  200 m of the start or finish, along the route or in a straight line, plus up
  to 100 m more that varies per route. It also cannot share a route that never
  gets far enough from its start. A lawyer still needs to read the page.
