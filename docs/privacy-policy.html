Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 30, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Wockett ("the App", "We", "Us") handles your information when you use our iOS application. We are committed to being transparent and collecting only what is necessary to make the app work.

Summary

Wockett is a local-first app. We do not operate servers of our own, do not create user accounts, and do not sell or share your personal data. Your data lives on your device and, if you use iCloud, syncs between your devices through your own private iCloud storage — where we cannot see it. The only information that ever becomes visible to other people is community content you explicitly choose to publish (see "Community content" below). Some features use Apple's own services (Maps, Weather, Health, iCloud) — those interactions are governed by Apple's Privacy Policy.

What We Access and Why

Data Purpose Stored or shared?
Location (while using the app) Generate nearby walking route suggestions; track your position during navigation; calculate distance walked Your live position is used on-device only during active walks. Route generation and street-level previews send coordinate data to Apple's Maps services — no personal information is included. Wockett itself never receives or stores your location. A GPS track may be saved temporarily to your device (see Walk Resume below).
Walk history Remember completed walks so you can view your history and repeat favourite routes Stored on your device (route name, date, distance, duration, GPS waypoints) and synced between your devices through your own private iCloud database via Apple's CloudKit. This private database belongs to your Apple Account — Wockett (the developer) cannot access or read it. Nothing is transmitted to us.
Walk resume Allow you to pick up a walk where you left off if the app is closed mid-session Your walk progress (including GPS track points) is temporarily saved to your device for up to 4 hours, then automatically deleted. This data never leaves your device.
Local notifications Water break reminders and scheduled walk reminders Reminders are local notifications only — no server is involved. Wockett never sends you marketing or remote push notifications. Apple's iCloud service does deliver silent, invisible background signals to keep your own data in sync between your devices; these are handled entirely by Apple.
HealthKit Read your daily step count to track goal progress; write completed workouts (distance, duration, calories, GPS route) to Apple Health Step count is read from your Health app. Completed walks and rides are written to Apple Health on your device. No Health data is transmitted to Wockett or any third party.
Weather (via Apple WeatherKit) Display weather conditions for suggested routes Fetched using your approximate location by Apple's WeatherKit service. Governed by Apple's Privacy Policy.
Community content (optional) Share routes with the community, join challenges, and post to the achievement feed Only if you choose to publish: shared route names and paths, challenge entries, and feed posts are stored in Apple's public CloudKit database and are visible to other Wockett users. Nothing is published automatically — every share is an explicit action. You can report or block content from other users, and community text is filtered before publishing. Your walk history, health data, and live location are never part of community content.

You can revoke any of these permissions at any time in iOS Settings → Wockett.

Data We Do Not Collect

  • We do not collect your name, email address, or any contact information
  • We do not create or require user accounts
  • We do not use analytics or crash-reporting SDKs
  • We do not use advertising or tracking frameworks
  • We do not collect photos, video, or camera data
  • We do not operate any servers of our own — on-device storage, sync, and community content all use Apple's iCloud/CloudKit infrastructure, governed by Apple's Privacy Policy

Third-Party Services

Wockett uses the following Apple platform services. All are governed by Apple's Privacy Policy:

  • Apple Maps (MapKit) — used for route generation, on-screen directions, and street-level Look Around previews. Coordinate data is sent to Apple's servers to power these features.
  • Apple WeatherKit — provides weather conditions based on your approximate location.
  • Apple HealthKit — step count is read from and completed workouts are written to your Health app. This data is not shared with any third party.
  • Apple iCloud / CloudKit — syncs your walk history, badges, and streaks between your own devices through your private iCloud database, and stores community content you choose to publish in a shared public database. Wockett cannot read your private database.

Children's Privacy

Wockett is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the App, please contact us and we will take steps to address it.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated date. Continued use of the App after changes are posted constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
