Getting started

Wockett has five tabs: Health, Routes, Home, Community and Settings. Home is where every walk starts.

Your first walk

Pick an activity, start it, and finish when you're done.

  1. On Home, under Start an activity, choose Walk, Run, Ride or Indoor.
  2. Tap Start Walk (or Run / Ride). The walk screen opens and tracking begins.
  3. Need a break? Tap Pause, then Resume when you're moving again.
  4. To finish, press and hold Hold to finish for one second.
  5. Choose Finish and save. (Or Keep walking if you held it by mistake.)
  6. Your summary shows distance, splits, records and your crew's progress. Tap Done.
The first time, Wockett asks for Apple Health (for your steps) and Location (for the map). Notifications start quiet; turn on full alerts any time in Settings → Notifications → Turn On Alerts.

Walking your dog

Add your pets once, and they earn credit for every walk they join.

Add a pet

  1. On Home, tap Add your dog in the Crew section (or Manage if you already have pets).
  2. Tap Add a Pet.
  3. Enter a Name and choose a Type: Dog, Cat, Rabbit, Bird or Other.
  4. Optionally add a breed, an emoji and a colour. Set a Daily step goal, or tap the suggestion for their breed.
  5. Tap Save.
Walking someone else's dog? Turn on This pet has an owner to notify and add the owner's name and phone. Like the rest of the pet's profile, it's kept on your devices and in your private iCloud, and never sent to us.

Choose who comes along

  1. In My Pets, switch on each pet that usually walks with you.
  2. During a walk, open More and tap a pet under Crew on this walk to add or remove them.
  3. A pet added mid-walk gets credit from the moment they join.
  4. To text the owner, tap Send the owner an update during the walk.

See your pet's progress

  1. Each walking pet has a ring on Home. Tap it for their last 7 days.
  2. Your walk summary shows Your crew's progress today.
  3. Health → Insights shows each pet's weekly and monthly totals.
Pet steps are worked out from the distance they walked with you, not from Apple Health.

Challenges

Set a goal, pick a deadline, and see who gets there first on the leaderboard.

Create a challenge

  1. Open the Community tab and tap Start a challenge.
  2. Give it a Challenge name and pick an Icon.
  3. Choose a Goal type: Steps, Distance or Pace.
  4. For Distance or Pace, choose the Activity: Any, Walk, Run or Ride.
  5. Set the goal: a step count, a distance, or a target pace and how many sessions it takes.
  6. Pick a Duration of 3, 7, 14 or 30 days.
  7. Tap Create Challenge.

Join and track a challenge

  1. In Community, open Challenges and tap one, or tap Join on the card.
  2. Tap Join & Sync Steps (or Distance / Runs).
  3. Come back and tap Sync My Steps to update your progress on the Leaderboard.
Challenges are open to the whole Wockett community. You need to be signed in to iCloud.

Share a challenge

  1. Open a challenge from Community → Challenges.
  2. Tap Share Challenge, under the join button.
  3. Pick Messages, Mail, WhatsApp or any app in the share sheet. Wockett writes the invitation for you, with the goal, how long it runs and a link.
  4. When a friend taps the link, the challenge opens in Wockett, ready to join. Don't have Wockett yet? The link shows a page with a button to get it.
Anyone can share a challenge, not just the person who made it. Distances and paces use your units, miles or kilometres.

Making routes

Build your own route on the map, save a walk you just did, or let Wockett find one for you.

Build a route on the map

  1. On Home, tap My Routes, then +.
  2. Tap the map to add waypoints. Wockett follows real paths between them.
  3. Choose Walk, Run or Ride, and turn on Loop to end where you started. Undo removes the last point.
  4. Tap Save Route, give it a name, and tap Save.

Save a walk as a route

  1. After a walk, on the summary screen, tap Save as Custom Route.
  2. Type a name and tap Save. It's now in My Routes.

Walk a saved route

  1. Open My Routes and tap the route.
  2. Pick Walk, Run or Ride, then tap Start in the bar at the bottom of the screen.
  3. Under the route's name you'll see its distance, time, climb and difficulty, with a line saying what made it easy or hard.
  4. From the same screen you can view its history, open it in Apple Maps, or share it to the community. Sharing shows a map of exactly what will be posted before you tap Share.

Let Wockett find a route

  1. Open the Routes tab and choose Routes or Trails.
  2. Pick Walk, Run or Ride and how long you have: Finish Goal, 20, 30 or 45 min.
  3. Tap Find Routes, look through the suggestions, pick one, and tap Start in the bar under the list.
  4. Tap the bookmark on a route to keep it.
Want trails from another state? Tap Trail regions and offline data under the Trails list (or go to Settings → About → Trail Regions) and tap Download. A download keeps trail lines and details on your phone; the map, directions and weather still need a connection. Use Filters above the list for loops only, surface, length or trails marked no dogs.

FAQ & troubleshooting

My steps show 0

Wockett reads steps from Apple Health. Check that Wockett has Health access in the Health app under Sharing. Still stuck? In Settings → Tracking, switch Data source to App Only and back to Apple Health.

I got the "untracked walk" notification hours later

iOS only lets Wockett check in the background a few times a day, so this notice arrives after your walk, not during it. If Wockett is open, Home offers to start tracking straight away.

Challenges or the community won't load

Challenges, shared routes and the achievement feed need you to be signed in to iCloud in the iPhone Settings app.

Will my walks move to a new iPhone?

Yes. Your history syncs through your own private iCloud, so signing in with the same Apple Account brings it back.

Routes says my location is off

Wockett plans routes from where you are, so with Location off it tells you before you search. Tap Choose a starting place and search for an address, park or station: Find Routes and Trails then work from there. Or tap Plan on the map to draw a route yourself, or turn Location back on in the iPhone Settings app. If you start a route that begins far from you, Wockett asks first.

I pressed Finish by accident

Finishing needs a one-second hold, then a confirmation. Tap Keep walking and nothing is lost.

Where is Wockett available?

Wockett is free on the App Store for iPhone and iPad. If you can't find it, it may not be offered in your country's App Store yet: email support@wockett.app and tell us where you are.
