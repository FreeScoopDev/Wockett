# Wockett

Wockett is a free iPhone and iPad app for walking, running and cycling, made
for people who walk with their dogs. It suggests routes and trails near you,
tracks each walk, run or ride on a map, and gives every pet who comes along
credit for the steps, with goals and rings of their own. Your history stays
on your devices and in your own private iCloud; challenges, shared routes and
the community feed are there if you want them.

- Website and help: [wockett.app](https://wockett.app)
- App Store: [Wockett](https://apps.apple.com/app/id6794364736)
- Privacy policy: [wockett.app/privacy-policy.html](https://wockett.app/privacy-policy.html)
- Support: [support@wockett.app](mailto:support@wockett.app)

Built in Swift with Apple's frameworks only, with no third-party dependencies.
