Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 24, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Wockett ("the App", "We", "Us") handles your information when you use our iOS application. We are committed to being transparent and collecting only what is necessary to make the app work.

Summary

Wockett is a local-first app. We do not operate servers, do not create user accounts, and do not sell or share your personal data. All data stays on your device except where noted below.

What We Access and Why

Data Purpose Stored or shared?
Location (while using the app) Generate nearby walking route suggestions; track your position during in-app navigation sessions; calculate distance walked Not stored between sessions. During navigation, your live position is used on-device only. GPS coordinates are sent to OpenTopoData for elevation data only — no personal information is included.
Walk history Remember completed walks so you can view your history and repeat favourite routes Stored on-device only (route name, date, distance, duration). Never transmitted or shared.
Notification access Send you a reminder at the date and time you choose when scheduling a future walk Reminders are local notifications only — no server is involved. Wockett never sends push notifications or uses your device token.
HealthKit step count Track your daily step goal progress Read only. Never leaves your device. We do not write to or modify your Health data.
Camera ARKit body tracking to count squat repetitions Processed on-device in real time. No images, video, or body data are recorded, stored, or transmitted.
Weather (via Apple WeatherKit) Display weather conditions for suggested routes Fetched using your approximate location by Apple's WeatherKit service. Governed by Apple's Privacy Policy.

You can revoke any of these permissions at any time in iOS Settings → Wockett.

Data We Do Not Collect

  • We do not collect your name, email address, or any contact information
  • We do not create or require user accounts
  • We do not use analytics or crash-reporting SDKs
  • We do not use advertising or tracking frameworks
  • We do not collect photos or video from your camera or photo library

Third-Party Services

  • OpenTopoData — receives GPS coordinate pairs to return elevation data. No personal information is transmitted. See their policy at opentopodata.org.
  • Apple WeatherKit — provides weather data based on approximate location. Governed by Apple's privacy policy.
  • Apple HealthKit — step count is read from your Health app. We do not share this data with any third party.

Children's Privacy

Wockett is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the App, please contact us and we will take steps to address it.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated date. Continued use of the App after changes are posted constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
